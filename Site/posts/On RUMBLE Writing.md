I thought I might start a RUMBLE series in a written format.

I suppose we'll see how it goes, but I'm hopeful. This first post is just a quick introduction to the format and an explanation of why I think RUMBLE writing has untapped potential.

*And so...*

### The Format

I intend to release short-to-medium length pieces — ranging from blog posts to essays — about once a week. I probably won't write a lot more than that. Each will cover a unique aspect of RUMBLE, its community, or something adjacent to it; for example, I plan to kick things off with some brief shiftstone articles to *finally* revamp the content in my [shiftstones video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9U4Fcgv7Eg).

My goal is to create a body of easily-referenced and approachable learning/discussion material, so I won't write many opinion pieces. I'll focus instead on summarizing core mechanics or summarizing whole discussions around controversial subjects.

I also might release technical articles: how I make <small muted=_>(made?)</small> videos, or how this website works under the hood (it's actually pretty cool!), or how RUMBLE mods work... and so on. You get the idea. Anyway:

### Why not make a video?

I'm glad you — *shockingly presumptuous* reader — asked! I'm doing this instead of videos because making videos is **really f<small style='color: #aaaaaa;'>(li)</small>cking slow**. Even if I dedicated 4 hours a day to it, I wouldn't get more than 20-30 minutes of video released a week. So, when I've got *maybe* an hour a day, writing is gonna be a lot more efficient. It's also a quicker format for **you** to digest. Reading doesn't take as long as watching a video. Sure, it's not as idle, but y'all can handle reading an article. <small muted=_><i>Right?</i></small>

And taking a wider view, RUMBLE is pretty content starved. I don't think that's a controversial opinion. We've got, like, one good Youtuber and a handful of barely-active mid ones (including myself!). Written content is in a similar boat: we've got Shoeless gradings, Bob news, and... that's it. It's worth growing and formalizing that space. A written tutorial/explanation series would be *amazingly* beneficial to the community: a *"Welcome to RUMBLE!"* post to show new players; a shiftstone series, a fundamentals series, and an overview of playstyles — all like I tried to accomplish with my channel. Of course, that didn't work out, but it'd be a whole lot easier here. Especially with the power of hindsight and 30,000 more bp under my belt.

This might be a leap for some of you, so let me give some background.

I'm a programmer. I read a *ton* of blog posts and articles about software. I use [RSS feeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS) and news aggregators (like the wonderful [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/)). I probably spend more time reading personal blogs on my phone than I spend using <i data-tooltip='This is hyperbole.'>all the other features of my phone combined</i>. Because of that, I often get frustrated wanting some RUMBLE content to read. So of course — to me at least — this was a gaping hole to be filled.
